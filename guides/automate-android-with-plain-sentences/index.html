Automate your Android phone with plain sentences

Tell Atom what should happen and when, in one sentence. Time-based requests become scheduled tasks; “when this happens” requests become automations. Both run in the background and report back.

Updated 27 November 20266 min readWorks with Atom 4.0.5 and later

Two kinds of automation

  • Scheduled tasks run a request at set times: Every weekday at 7:45, give me my calendar and anything urgent in my notifications.
  • Automations react to something happening on the phone: When I plug in the charger after 11 PM, turn on Do Not Disturb.

Scheduled tasks

Ask in chat and Atom creates the task with a name, the request and a schedule, then tells you when it runs next. Schedules can be written the way you’d say them:

daily 21:00
weekdays 07:45
weekly mon,thu 09:00
every 2h
in 30m
cron 0 9 * * 1-5

Each task has its own settings:

  • Notify: every run, only when the result changes, or silently to the archive.
  • Capabilities: what the run may use, from web, reading the device, controlling the device, the terminal, MCP tools and operating apps. By default a task gets web and device reading only.
  • Quiet hours: hold runs between 23:00 and 07:30.
  • Model: the model for scheduled tasks, or a different one per task.
  • Where results go: a new chat per run, or one conversation you can follow up in.

Manage them from Scheduled Tasks in the side menu: run now, pause, stop or delete. Runs missed while the phone was off can catch up.

For runs on the minute, tap the health banner on the Scheduled Tasks screen to allow exact alarms and unrestricted battery use. Without them, Android may delay runs by several minutes.

Automations

Describe an automation in one sentence and Atom builds it once into a trigger, optional conditions and steps. It shows you the result, with a battery estimate and any conflicts with your other automations, and asks before saving. When the event fires, no model runs unless one of the steps asks for it.

PartWhat you can use
TriggersA time or schedule; charger connected or disconnected; battery level; screen on or off; unlocked; Wi-Fi or Bluetooth connected or disconnected; headphones plugged or unplugged; airplane mode or Do Not Disturb changed; arriving at or leaving a place; a notification from an app or containing a word; an app opened; the phone starting up
ConditionsA time window, days of the week, battery level, charging, a Wi-Fi network, rooted
StepsRun the agent with a request (optionally waiting until charging or idle), run one tool, show a notification, wait, stop unless a condition holds

Agent steps can use details of the event, such as the app, title and text of the notification that fired it. To change an automation, open Settings → Automations. Triggers that need a permission (location, background location, Bluetooth or notification access) ask for it in the automation editor.

Sentences to try

  • When I plug in the charger after 11 PM, turn on Do Not Disturb.
  • When I get a notification from my bank, summarise it for me.
  • When the battery drops below 20%, lower the brightness and tell me.
  • Every Sunday at 19:30, list large unused apps and downloads I could remove.
  • Every day at 10:00, check the price of this product and tell me only if it drops.

Ready-made routines in 4.0.6

Atom 4.0.6 adds routines you can save with one tap from the Scheduled Tasks screen: Morning brief, Price watch, Weekly phone cleanup and Evening wrap-up. Each opens filled in, so you only need to tap Save. See the changelog.
